Anti-Ghost Playbook

Accountability Pairs
Assign at Session 1. Each pair sends a check-in Teams message before each session. Personal beats group for accountability.
Rotating Roles
Note-taker posts 5-bullet Confluence recap within 24hrs. Activity lead runs the Lucidchart exercise with you. Rotate every session.
Exit Ticket
Every session ends with each person typing 1 takeaway into Confluence or Lucidchart. 60 seconds. Creates participation record.
Personal Outreach
If someone misses a session, DM them — not a group note. "Hey, missed you today, here's what we covered [link]." Takes 2 minutes.
Session 5 Pulse Check
Halfway point Outlook poll: Are you getting value? Is the time commitment realistic? What's one thing we should change?

Building Toward Experts

Sessions 1–3
Watch for who asks the best questions and goes deeper than the pre-work. These are your future co-facilitators.
Sessions 4–5
Invite 1–2 technical people to co-present the Concept Anchor. Frame it as: "I'd love your take — can you take 5 minutes?"
Sessions 6–10
Formally recruit a co-facilitator. Share prep. By Session 8 they lead, you manage logistics.

Hybrid Session Tips

→Share Lucidchart link in Teams chat before the session starts — remote and in-person both click in simultaneously.
→Designate one in-person person as "remote advocate" — they flag when a remote participant has their hand up.
→Record sessions. Post the link to Confluence with the session recap so people who miss can catch up.
→For the Concept Anchor, screen-share Confluence — it becomes the shared visual anchor for in-person and remote equally.
→Exit ticket works better in Confluence comments than verbally — everyone types simultaneously.
